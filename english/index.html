常用表達 Expression2026.09.14

have time on one's hands

I had time on my hands 是什麼意思？

中文意思

表示「有空閒時間」，通常暗示暫時沒有事情需要處理。

I had some time on my hands, so I started learning English.

語感補充

比單純的 I had time 更強調手邊正好多出一段空閒。

單字 Vocabulary2026.09.14

pandemic

/pænˈdem.ɪk/ · noun / adjective

中文意思

（疾病的）全球大流行；也可作形容詞。

The pandemic changed the way many people work.

易混淆字

epidemic 通常指特定地區內的流行；pandemic 則跨越多個國家或洲。

常用表達 Expression2026.09.29

take advantage of + 人／機會／情況

如何正確使用 take advantage of？

核心意思

take advantage of + 機會／情況：善用、把握，通常是正面或中性。

take advantage of + 人：利用、占某人便宜，通常帶負面意思。

We should take advantage of this opportunity to expand our business.

He took advantage of her kindness.

更多例句與常見錯誤
  • Let's take advantage of the good weather and go hiking.
  • The company took advantage of new technology to reduce costs.
  • Don't let anyone take advantage of you.

錯：take advantage from an opportunity
對：take advantage of an opportunity

注意：這裡的 advantage 前面通常不加 an。

文法 Grammar2026.09.14

How exactly is X affecting Y?

How exactly is climate change affecting extreme weather?

句型結構

How exactly + be + 主詞 + V-ing + 受詞?

詢問某件事「具體究竟如何」正在影響另一件事。exactly 加強 how，要求更精確的說明。

How exactly is climate change affecting extreme weather?

更多例句
  • How exactly is AI changing the way we work?
  • How exactly is the new policy affecting small businesses?
  • How exactly are higher interest rates affecting home buyers?
